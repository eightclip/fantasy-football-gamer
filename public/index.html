Good habits

Setting your lineup

Look at the whole week, not just Sunday.

Thursday, Saturday and Monday games lock earlier. Handle those players first.

A healthy fill-in is worth more than an empty spot.

Any points beat zero. If you cannot find a starter, start whoever is playing.

Check again the morning of.

Projections and injury news keep changing until kickoff.

Waivers and free agents

Pick up players for the weeks ahead.

A big game last week matters less than a bigger role going forward.

Rotate kickers and defenses by matchup.

They are hard to predict, so play the best matchup each week instead of holding one all season.

Backups to star running backs are cheap insurance.

If your starter gets hurt, his backup often takes over the job.

Injuries

Practice reports come first.

A player who misses practice all week is far less likely to play than one who was limited.

Out means zero.

Swap an Out player even if his game is late in the week.

Trades

Offers that help both teams get accepted.

Trade from a position you are deep at for one you are thin at.

Think about the playoff weeks.

A player with easy matchups late in the season is worth more than his projection today.
